A capstone tying the whole series together: download GBIF data, clean it, check fitness for use, integrate it with other sources, produce a figure and document it reproducibly (with a light look at Quarto) — and cite everything.

An optional data-integration and workflow deep-dive workshop follows this session. The date is announced with the session invitation.

Data Fika sessions are free, online and open to everyone — 20–30 minutes of
presentation followed by about 15 minutes of hands-on work and Q&A. Email
[gbif@nrm.se](mailto:gbif@nrm.se) to be added to the invitation list, and see the full
programme on the [Data Fika page](/data-fika/).
