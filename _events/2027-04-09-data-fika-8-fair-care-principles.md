FAIR as practical implementation — the spine that runs through the whole series — and CARE as a distinct governance framework: who decides, and the history of collections, grounded in Swedish collections' own holdings and Sámi data governance.

An optional CARE discussion (about one hour) with invited speakers accompanies this session. The date is announced with the session invitation.

Data Fika sessions are free, online and open to everyone — 20–30 minutes of
presentation followed by about 15 minutes of hands-on work and Q&A. Email
[gbif@nrm.se](mailto:gbif@nrm.se) to be added to the invitation list, and see the full
programme on the [Data Fika page](/data-fika/).
