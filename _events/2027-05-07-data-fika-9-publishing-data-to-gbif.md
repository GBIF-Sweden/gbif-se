The publishing workflow: how collection data becomes a published GBIF dataset — Darwin Core mapping, extensions, metadata and licensing choices — with a small look at how it works on the back end (the IPT and the star schema). Aimed especially at data stewards and publishers.

Data Fika sessions are free, online and open to everyone — 20–30 minutes of
presentation followed by about 15 minutes of hands-on work and Q&A. Email
[gbif@nrm.se](mailto:gbif@nrm.se) to be added to the invitation list, and see the full
programme on the [Data Fika page](/data-fika/).
