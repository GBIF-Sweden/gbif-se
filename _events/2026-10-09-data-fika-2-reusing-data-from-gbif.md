The spine of the series: getting data out of GBIF in a reproducible, citable way. We cover website downloads, rgbif, pygbif and the API, download DOIs, cubes, occurrence vs sample data, Darwin Core Archives and DwC-DP, and when to use SBDI vs GBIF.org.

An optional hands-on companion workshop follows this session: everyone pulls, filters and cites their own download. The date is announced with the session invitation.

Data Fika sessions are free, online and open to everyone — 20–30 minutes of
presentation followed by about 15 minutes of hands-on work and Q&A. Email
[gbif@nrm.se](mailto:gbif@nrm.se) to be added to the invitation list, and see the full
programme on the [Data Fika page](/data-fika/).
